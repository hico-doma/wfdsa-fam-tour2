Gyeongju – A Museum Without Walls

Discover Gyeongju, the thousand-year capital of the Silla Kingdom and host city of the 2025 APEC Summit. Experience a compact one-stop MICE destination where ancient heritage, modern meetings, leisure, dining, and accommodation come together.

UNESCO Heritage

Bulguksa Temple Guided Heritage Tour

A UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1995, Bulguksa Temple is the finest example of Silla Buddhist architecture. Explore its iconic stone bridges, Dabotap and Seokgatap pagodas, and discover the spiritual legacy of ancient Korea with a professional guide.

Day 2 · Oct 23 15:30–16:30 · Guided tour
APEC 2025 Legacy

Gyeongju Expo Grand Park APEC 2025 Legacy Hall

Home to the APEC 2025 Legacy Hall, Gyeongju Expo Grand Park pairs landmark modern architecture — including the iconic Gyeongju Tower — with sweeping gardens, showing off the city's contemporary face alongside its ancient heritage.

Day 2 · Oct 23 17:00–18:00 · Legacy Hall visit
Hotel Inspection

Hilton Gyeongju Bomun Lake · Accommodation

Located just minutes from HICO, Hilton Gyeongju offers international-standard accommodations, banquet facilities, and meeting services — an ideal hotel for corporate meetings, incentive groups, and international congresses.

Day 2 · Oct 23 18:00–18:30 · Hotel Inspection & overnight stay
Night Tour

Cheomseongdae Observatory Guided night tour

Built in the 7th century during the reign of Queen Seondeok, Cheomseongdae is the oldest surviving astronomical observatory in Asia and one of Gyeongju's most iconic landmarks.

Day 2 · Evening Near Wolseong & Donggung Palace
Night Tour

Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond Guided Night Tour

Originally a royal palace complex for Silla crown princes, Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond is renowned for its breathtaking nighttime scenery, where illuminated pavilions reflect beautifully across the water.

Day 2 · Oct 23 20:30–21:30 · Night tour
Venue Inspection

HICO Hwabaek International Convention Center

Located in the Bomun Tourism Complex, HICO is Gyeongju's premier convention center and a core venue of APEC 2025. Tour its convention halls, meeting rooms, and exhibition facilities designed for international events.

Day 3 · Oct 24 09:00–10:00 · Convention Center Inspection
Korea Unique Venue

Hwangnyongwon Unique Venue Inspection

Inspired by the legendary Hwangnyongsa Temple pagoda, this striking nine-story landmark offers a distinctive setting for receptions, gala dinners, and cultural events overlooking Bomun Lake.

Day 3 · Oct 24 10:00–11:00 · Unique Venue Inspection
Immersive Media Art

Flashback Gyerim

Experience Gyeongju's founding legends through cutting-edge media art. Projection mapping, light, and sound transform the sacred Gyerim Forest into an unforgettable immersive storytelling experience.

Day 3 · Oct 24 11:00–12:00 · Experience
Dining

GODOBEOL HANJUNGSIK Lunch

Set in the Sumeori food district, GODOBEOL HANJUNGSIK occupies two elegant hanok buildings — a main house and an annex — that recall a noble family estate, framed by a garden as striking as the architecture itself. The restaurant serves a refined hanjeongsik (traditional Korean course meal) built on fresh, seasonal ingredients and time-honored Korean cooking methods.

Day 3 · Oct 24 12:10–13:10 · Lunch
Silla Royal Tombs

Daereungwon Ancient Tomb Complex

Explore one of Gyeongju's most iconic heritage sites, where 23 royal tombs of the Silla Kingdom are preserved together. Visit Cheonmachong, the only excavated royal tomb open to the public.

Day 3 · Oct 24 13:20–14:30 · Final stop of the Fam Tour
