Chapter 2 · Oct 23–24

Gyeongju — a museum without walls, an APEC-proven host

In the afternoon of day two the tour moves to Gyeongju, capital of the Silla Kingdom for nearly a thousand years and host city of APEC 2025. Here, UNESCO World Heritage sites sit minutes from a modern international convention center — a setting no other destination can replicate.

* The above schedule is subject to change due to operational circumstances.

Landmark

Cheomseongdae 첨성대 · Astronomical Observatory

Built in the 7th century under Queen Seondeok, Cheomseongdae is the oldest surviving astronomical observatory in Asia — a quiet, elegant landmark and one of the most recognizable silhouettes in Gyeongju.

En route Near Wolseong & Donggung Palace
UNESCO Heritage

Bulguksa Temple 불국사 · Guided tour

Inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1995, Bulguksa is the masterpiece of Silla Buddhist architecture — the "Temple of the Buddha Land." Walk past the national-treasure stone bridges Cheongungyo and Baegungyo to the Dabotap and Seokgatap pagodas with a professional guide.

Oct 23 15:30–16:30 · Guided tour
Night Tour

Donggung Palace & Wolji Pond 동궁과 월지 · Guided night tour

The secondary palace of Silla crown princes, set around a pond designed to look as boundless as the sea. After dark, the illuminated pavilions reflected on the water make this Gyeongju's most celebrated night view — and the perfect close to your first evening in the ancient capital.

Oct 23 20:30–21:30 · Night tour

Unique Venue

HwangNyongWon 황룡원 · Unique venue inspection

A striking nine-story pagoda-style landmark inspired by the legendary Hwangnyongsa Temple pagoda, rising beside Bomun Lake. A one-of-a-kind venue for receptions and cultural programs that photographs like nowhere else in Korea.

Oct 24 10:00–11:00 · Unique Venue Inspection
Immersive Media Art

Flashback Gyerim 플래시백 계림

An immersive media-art experience set in and around Gyerim, the sacred "Rooster Forest" tied to Silla's founding legends. Cutting-edge projection and lighting bring thousand-year-old stories to life — heritage meets technology.

Oct 24 11:00–12:00 · Experience
Royal Tombs

Daereungwon 대릉원 · Ancient Tomb Complex

Twenty-three grass-covered royal tombs of the Silla Kingdom gathered in one serene park in the heart of the city — including Cheonmachong, the only tomb whose interior is open to visitors. The tour's unforgettable final stop.

Oct 24 13:20–14:30 · Final stop of the Fam Tour
